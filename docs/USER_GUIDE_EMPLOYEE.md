# HRMS Employee User Guide

This guide explains how employees can use the HRMS system for self-service tasks.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the HRMS login page
2. Enter your email and password provided by HR
3. Click **Sign In**

If you forgot your password, contact your HR administrator.

---

## Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your personal dashboard with:

- **Quick Stats**: Leave balance, attendance this month, pending requests
- **Quick Actions**: Clock in/out, apply leave, submit expense
- **Announcements**: Company-wide notices and updates
- **Upcoming Events**: Holidays, meetings, deadlines

---

## Attendance

### Marking Attendance

1. Go to **Attendance** from the sidebar
2. Click **Check In** when you start work
3. Click **Check Out** when you end work

The system records:
- Check-in/out times
- Total working hours
- Work location (if enabled)

### Viewing Attendance History

- View your attendance records for the current month
- See summary: Days present, absent, late arrivals
- Filter by date range

### Attendance Regularization

If you forgot to mark attendance:

1. Click **Regularization Request**
2. Select the date
3. Enter the correct in/out times
4. Provide a reason
5. Submit for manager approval

---

## Leave Management

### Checking Leave Balance

Your leave balances are displayed at the top of the Leaves page:
- Casual Leave
- Sick Leave
- Earned Leave
- Compensatory Off
- Unpaid Leave

### Applying for Leave

1. Go to **Leaves** > **Apply Leave**
2. Select leave type
3. Choose start and end dates
4. Select full day or half day (if applicable)
5. Enter reason for leave
6. Click **Submit**

Your manager will receive a notification to approve/reject.

### Leave Status

- **Pending**: Awaiting approval
- **Approved**: Leave is confirmed
- **Rejected**: Leave was not approved (check comments)
- **Cancelled**: You cancelled the request

### Cancelling Leave

1. Find the leave request in your list
2. Click the cancel button
3. Confirm cancellation

Note: Only pending or future approved leaves can be cancelled.

### Viewing Holidays

Go to **Leaves** > **Holidays** to see:
- Company holidays
- Optional holidays
- Restricted holidays

---

## Payroll

### Viewing Payslips

1. Go to **Payroll** from the sidebar
2. See list of all your payslips
3. Click on a payslip to view details:
   - Gross salary
   - Deductions (tax, insurance, etc.)
   - Net pay
   - Payment date

### Downloading Payslips

- Click **Download PDF** on any payslip
- Save for your records

### Yearly Summary

View your annual compensation summary:
- Total earnings
- Total deductions
- Tax paid (for Form 16/W2)

---

## Timesheets

If your company uses timesheet tracking:

### Logging Time

1. Go to **Timesheets**
2. Click **Add Entry**
3. Select the project
4. Enter hours worked
5. Add description of work done
6. Submit

### Weekly Timesheet View

- View your entire week's entries
- Total hours per project
- Total hours per day

### Submitting Timesheets

1. Complete all entries for the week
2. Click **Submit for Approval**
3. Your manager will review and approve

---

## Expenses

### Submitting an Expense Claim

1. Go to **Expenses** > **Submit Claim**
2. Select expense category:
   - Travel
   - Meals
   - Office Supplies
   - Training
   - Other
3. Enter amount and date
4. Upload receipt (required for most expenses)
5. Add description
6. Submit

### Tracking Expenses

View status of your expense claims:
- **Draft**: Not yet submitted
- **Pending**: Awaiting approval
- **Approved**: Will be reimbursed
- **Rejected**: Not approved
- **Paid**: Reimbursed to your account

---

## Performance

### My Goals

View and track your performance goals:

1. Go to **Performance**
2. See your assigned goals
3. Update progress percentage
4. Add notes on achievements

### Self-Appraisal

During appraisal season:

1. Go to **Performance** > **Appraisals**
2. Complete your self-evaluation
3. Rate yourself on competencies
4. Submit before deadline

### Giving Feedback

If 360-degree feedback is enabled:

1. Go to **Performance** > **Feedback**
2. See pending feedback requests
3. Provide ratings and comments for colleagues
4. Submit feedback

---

## Assets

### Viewing Assigned Assets

Go to **Assets** > **My Assets** to see:
- Company laptop
- Mobile phone
- ID card
- Access cards
- Other assigned equipment

Each asset shows:
- Asset name and tag number
- Assignment date
- Condition
- Return date (if applicable)

### Requesting an Asset

1. Click **Request Asset**
2. Select asset type
3. Provide justification
4. Submit request

---

## Helpdesk / Tickets

### Creating a Support Ticket

1. Go to **Helpdesk** > **Create Ticket**
2. Select category:
   - IT Support
   - HR Query
   - Facilities
   - Payroll Question
   - Other
3. Set priority (Low, Medium, High)
4. Describe your issue
5. Attach files if needed
6. Submit

### Tracking Tickets

View status of your tickets:
- **Open**: Being worked on
- **In Progress**: Assigned to someone
- **Resolved**: Issue fixed
- **Closed**: Completed

### Adding Comments

1. Open a ticket
2. Add additional information as comments
3. Support team will respond

---

## Travel Requests

### Submitting a Travel Request

1. Go to **Travel** > **New Request**
2. Enter trip details:
   - Purpose
   - Destination
   - Departure/Return dates
   - Mode of transport
   - Estimated costs
3. Submit for approval

### Travel Status

- **Pending**: Awaiting approval
- **Approved**: Book your travel
- **Rejected**: Not approved
- **Completed**: Trip finished (submit expenses)

## Profile

### Updating Personal Information

1. Go to **Profile** from the top-right menu
2. Update details:
   - Phone number
   - Emergency contact
   - Address
   - Bank details (for salary)
3. Save changes

Some fields may require HR approval to change.

### Changing Password

1. Go to **Profile** > **Change Password**
2. Enter current password
3. Enter new password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Confirm new password
5. Save

---

## Tips for Using HRMS

1. **Check in/out daily** - Maintain accurate attendance records
2. **Apply leave in advance** - Give your manager time to plan
3. **Keep receipts** - Always upload receipts for expense claims
4. **Update goals regularly** - Don't wait until appraisal time
5. **Check announcements** - Stay informed about company updates

If you need assistance:

3. **Contact HR** for policy questions
4. **Ask your manager** for approval-related queries
